# Model card

Model version: `mdt-20261016-174a69a1`. Trained at `2026-10-16T03:13:12.134495+00:00`.
Metrics are generated by `python -m app.ml.train` and recorded in
[the machine-readable report](../backend/artifacts/progression_model.metrics.json).

## Intended use and release status

Exploratory research and software demonstration of a versioned myopia twin.
This model has no demonstrated clinical utility. It is not a medical device,
diagnosis, prescription, treatment-selection system or causal model. Do not use
the public free-host demo for actual patient data or clinical decisions.

## Verified input and missing information

Only [Zenodo DIMS 36-month data](https://zenodo.org/records/18166476), CC BY 4.0,
is used: 20 subjects, 140 scheduled visits, baseline through month 36. See the
[data card](DATA_CARD.md) for the exact workbook columns, hashes and all missingness.
The two requested Dryad workbooks could not be verified and are excluded.

SE/AL are repeated measurements; recorded age exists only at baseline. Calendar
dates, parental myopia, visual acuity, corneal curvature, behaviour and ergonomics
are missing. Gender B/G has no supplied codebook and normalized sex stays missing.
SER is interpreted as diopters and AL as millimeters from names/context/ranges;
no independent units or eye-selection codebook was published. No conversion,
calendar date, advancing age or exposure is invented. All subjects wear DIMS;
this cannot identify a treatment effect or support other treatment populations.

## Protocol

- A single feature builder is used for training, live twins and simulation.
  Every feature uses history available at the index visit; future measurements
  provide targets only. Unknown measurements remain NaN in the deployed estimator.
- Targets are future SE/AL levels at 6 and 12 months, using the nearest recorded
  follow-up within 2.75 months. Each target has 60 labeled rows: 24 fitting,
  30 calibration, six test rows. Repeated visits are correlated observations.
- The patient-grouped fitting/calibration/test split is **8/10/2 subjects**.
  Tests prove split disjointness. No row-level split or test-set tuning is used.
- Six fixed point-model candidates per target: carry-forward, mean change,
  regularized/smooth/flexible change-HGB, and the previous absolute-level HGB.
  Three shuffled four-fold grouped CV runs (seeds 41/42/43) use only the eight
  fitting subjects. Lowest mean fold MAE selects the point model. The same-fold
  development scores after selection are optimistic, not nested unbiased CV.
- Measured baseline + predicted change avoids forcing eight fitting patients to
  represent every absolute eye size. SE selects smooth change-HGB (100 iterations,
  learning rate .06, three leaves, minimum leaf six, L2 30). AL selects training
  mean change. Per-target candidate scores are retained in the generated report.
- Separate .05/.95 HGB quantile models forecast change and add the measured
  baseline (100 iterations, .06 learning rate, seven leaves, minimum leaf three,
  L2 10, no early stopping). If a live anchor is absent, an explicitly trained
  absolute-level HGB fallback receives the original NaNs. It invents no input
  measurement; missing-anchor predictions and intervals are not separately validated.
- Split-conformal calibration uses the maximum nonconformity across all labeled
  visits per calibration patient and a conservative finite-sample quantile.
  Its nominal target is 90% simultaneous patient coverage under exchangeability;
  this does not guarantee observed coverage in two test patients. Served bands
  also include the point estimate, and evaluation uses those same served bands.
- Percentile 95% intervals use 500 patient-cluster bootstrap samples, retaining
  each sampled subject's repeated visits. Two subjects are far too few for
  reliable bootstrap inference; extreme negative R-squared bounds are retained.

## Existing fixed benchmark and baselines

Lower errors are better. All baselines use the identical patient split. Ridge
uses training-only median imputation/scaling with alpha 10; Random Forest uses
200 trees/minimum leaf three. Imputation is internal to comparison baselines;
it never fills source records or creates exposures. Optional XGBoost/LightGBM
were not installed and no results are claimed for them.

## Unavailable capabilities and remaining validation

The clinical ablation reproduces the main errors; +behaviour and +ergonomics are
explicitly not evaluable because measurements are absent. No risk classifier is
released because independent labels are insufficient. `supported_scenario_inputs`
is empty: numeric behaviour, ergonomic and treatment comparisons/sweeps are
rejected, including through the agent. An unchanged baseline can still be shown.
Attribution describes the fitted model, not causation; feature ablation is used
when local SHAP is unavailable. Global permutation importance is descriptive on
the tiny holdout and did not select features or tune the model.

Multi-year rollouts feed predictions back into the model and use heuristic
uncertainty after the first step. There is no external validation cohort, fairness
assessment, calibrated long-term clinical risk or evidence for treatment effects.
Obtain and inspect the larger Dryad workbook, resolve units/eye linkage, expand
the cohort and repeat a locked external evaluation before making performance
claims. See [benchmarks and source-paper context](BENCHMARKS.md).
