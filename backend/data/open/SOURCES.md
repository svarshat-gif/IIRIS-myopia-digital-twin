https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18166476 (published 6 January 2026).

Credit: Siti Nurdiana Yusop, Bariah Mohd-Ali, Nur Adibah Yahaya,
Syarifah Faiza Syed Mohd Dardin, Mizhanim Mohamad Shahimin, Pui Juan Woi.
Title: Raw data for: Association between peripheral refraction, axial elongation,
and myopia progression in children wearing DIMS lenses over 36 months.
Repository metadata is included. No modifications to the workbook.
Normalization creates a separate table, preserves missing values and excludes
peripheral refraction. MD5: 57f458e340609a1cc65f5b2083637d18.
SHA256: 02039445b4d4e540aea5a5a16f02134e5929d92074ecba0a5e85daf6d6dda701.
