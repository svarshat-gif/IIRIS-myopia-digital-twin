# Additional research inputs

OLSM: aplore3 0.9, Luca Braglia; data attributed to Hosmer, Lemeshow and
Sturdivant, Applied Logistic Regression (3rd ed., Wiley, 2013).
Source archive, original R data, DESCRIPTION/codebook and full GPL-3 license
are retained in olsm/. CRAN repository MD5 is verified. myopia.csv is a derived
text serialization; rdata 1.0.0 conversion preserves the source values/category
labels. All 618 rows match the Kaggle Myopia Study after documented category
recoding. The Kaggle copy's Unknown license is not used as a redistribution basis.
GPL-3 terms apply to the included upstream material.

Aier-SERI: Pan et al., A psychometric evaluation of the Chinese Impact of Vision
Impairment questionnaire in an adult cohort with high myopia using Rasch analysis,
PLOS One, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12510582/ . The article explicitly
links the Kaggle release and confirms CC0. S3 Data.xlsx is unmodified. The eye-side
codebook ambiguity is preserved; this source is baseline reference, not forecasts.

Miopia: Manuel de Prado Garcia, https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/manueldepradogarcia/miopia .
Uploader metadata specifies CC BY-NC 4.0. Original CSVs are unmodified; derived
research tables are separate. Noncommercial research only; source measurement
protocol/scales still need clarification. No progression training or clinical seed.

Exact local SHA256 fingerprints are in research_sources.json. The OLSM archive
also has a verified repository MD5. Synthetic lifestyle data and unresolved-license
Kaggle/Dryad candidates are excluded. Do not claim these are DIMS follow-up visits.
